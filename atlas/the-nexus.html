The Nexus

The land below, another world underneath the surface... a subterranean realm of dark mystery, forbidden and mystical domains, and endless war. The Nexus is so named due to the presence of the Leylines - tremendous magical 'rivers' where the Infinite breaks down into its base components. The Leylines form a vast network of interconnected channels that seem to run like veins far underneath. It is said that the Leylines converge deep below the legendary Kingdom of Aghoras. Truly, we don't entirely know the extent of how vast the Nexus sprawls - many scholars have pieced together its landscape from explorer's lore, historical documents, and stories that have reached the surface. Few maps exist of its cavernous depths, though the few that do have shocked even the most open-minded cartographer.

There are an unknown number of entrances that truly descend deep enough to enter the lands below. Many travelers claim caverns and caves are the Nexus, which they almost always are not. The Nexus is deep - deeper than all believe - and when one steps upon it, it is more than evident. Some entrances are said to be heavily secured by military guard within great kingdoms, while others are far out in the dangerous wilderness.

Of the Nexus, also commonly called the Underworld, or the Depths, great wonders exist, and great horrors have grown. Of common knowledge, we know much of the Demonic Lords, who retreated below the surface at the beginning of the Third Era. In their seething agony of existence and their ultimate defeat in the Battle of Dawn, the Demonic remnants of the ashborne nearly went extinct. They were saved by the deepfolk, subterranean natives of pale and gray skin. The Scribes of Valoris believe that many centuries after their initial friendship, the cultures went to war for a mysterious reason. It seems it was started sometime after the Great Collapse, the calamitous event at the end of the Third Era when the Leylines went "dry" - a global mana drought, fallout of Godfall. What is known, is that from this initial conflict, hatred was born that has since not been quelled. The ashborne defeated the deepfolk and enslaved them. Their brutal oppression granted them the construction of a great city, which became a nation by might; Yn'rylith, the Ashen Kingdom. The Demonic Lords, once again, rose from the ashes of utter loss, by malice and cruelty. The deepfolk continue to be subjugated, though they resist as they can. Now, the Demonic Lords even fight one another, in the ever ongoing conflict now known as the Spawn Wars.

From accounts of texts uncovered, the Demonic ashborne split into a dozen or more distinct 'houses', which generally have waged silent war through political intrigue and manipulation. Now, however, the wars have grown and the Lords fight to protect their lands with loyal knights and magical potency. The houses of the ashborne have historically been secretive and insulated - little is truly known to us on the surface, yet it is presumed it is related to lineage and ancestry of the original Demonic Kings of the Dark Age.

The ashborne have been noted as vicious and callous, but also unnaturally beautiful. Their skin tones range from gray to pale, as ash, and their eyes a range of colors - with their pupils sometimes looking as if there is a flame alight in them. Their hair is grayscale. Common foods of the ashborne are unknown.

Before the ashborne came, the deepfolk were largely unencountered. Some histories spoke of people below the surface, but few knew truly the culture and the extent of their existence. It seems to be, that from an unknown beginning, the deepfolk dwelled among the leylines, and lived amongst themselves. The leylines granted them immense power, and from it, they were infused with raw mana. With this power, they sprawled underneath the lands, and traces of their ancient history can be found in deep caverns in all nine continents. However, when the Deep Withering came, they, who were born and accustomed to the incredible presence of the magic, grew weak and withered.

The deepfolk, to a surface dweller, might seem as if they are visually quite similar to the ashborne, yet, they are extraordinarily different. The deepfolk are gray skinned, but with pigment more saturated - some say their skin can range even toward deep blue or dark purple. Their eyes are large, giving them a much more inhuman facial proportion. To add to their alien visage, their eyes have been stated to be a singular color, any color, without whites of the eyes. Their hair is white, and they are mostly hairless on their body. They are generally tall and thin. Legends say that they were once not like this, but the opposite - a massive, imposing race, that dominated the true largest continent of Elorae - the lands below.

Without a true contiguous map, it is hard to describe the locales of the Nexus, but, according to well-trusted scholars and explorers, it is unable to be comprehended. Simply put - it is not just caverns and caves where strange people dwell. It is another realm - a world, with cities, roads, fields, deserts, oceans, mountains, and even its own sky and weather patterns. There are famous accounts of the Deep Desert, a massive region where the sky above looks as if it is filled with stars that glitter on the sands, the Silver Forest, a great woodland that shimmers with great trees of leaves that shine like metal, a neverending land of water and rain labeled as the "Auracine" in ancient texts, and the Infernal Pits, seas of fire whence came Izil, the Burning God.
