Mintas Fae

Lord of the Everdark

  • Dark Spirit, Everdark King, Gloomlord

“Gods’ splinter soaks in the river of dreams. Uyin has fallen, Uyin has risen. As shall we. Whilst the Red Moon shall rise, to bless us all.”

— Mintas

(MINN-tahss FAY)

Lord of the Everdark

Great demonic lord from the underworld. Lord of the Everdark, below the green domain, where the shadows linger like fog. When the Black Lord came from the west during the Gloom, from beyond the inferno, the Dark Spirit arose to listen. Now, the sylvan groves rot and wither, shadows pool in deep places, and the umbral words etch themselves into the roots. The words of Fae are translations from the darkness. Thus, is spoken, after the transformation comes to pass, the night shall come, and by the grace of the moon shall we ascend.
