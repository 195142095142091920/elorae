(KAIR-uh-borr)

Ancient of the Light

Rogue Ancient said to have come from the Timeless Void to create the world. Creator of the first light, fire, the Suns, and the Infinite. Destroyed by Maegoroth during the calamitous Battle of the Dawn, as punishment for the Great Heresy.
