Saoirse

High Druidess of Arleth

  • Heartroot Sanctuary
  • Balamwood
  • Arlethian clergy
  • Bound to Aoife

“Mother of groves, weaver of wreaths, and maiden of flowers: let me be rooted in Your peace, woven by Your gentleness, and ever returned to the blooming path of all living things.”

— Saoirse's daily prayer to Arleth

Saoirse's entrance to the world came with the first flowers of the Balamwood's spring, or so she had always been told. She was born to prominent druids within a veiled Arlethian order and raised with their devotion to bestial magic and the healing arts.

The certainty of her path shifted with the arrival of Vaerek Rathkin. He became nearer to family than duty. When he was named her Wreath Warden, she could barely contain her joy.

She found an abandoned hatchling and named her Aoife. Through a ceremonial rite their spirits were bound as sisters.

When she saw signs of Shiokian infiltration she acted alone. Her soul was rescued from the Hunt at a cost that lingered. Prayer became a discipline rather than a comfort.

She invoked the Rite of Pilgrimage and left the Balamwood with Aoife, south toward Fala.
