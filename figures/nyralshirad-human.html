Nyralshirad Human Form

Dragonlord of the Great Wilds

  • Cursebane Vengeant, Greendrake, Bane of the Huntress, The Sunken King

“Sylvan rancor of thorn and vine, sunken deep, vengeant and moulding”

— Nyralshirad

(Nih-RALL-shih-RAHD)

Dragonlord of the Woodland

Dragonlord of the Kindred, born of sin. By his father’s command, was sent to dwell in the forest lands of Hesk. Within the gnarled forests of Athag’ir, the sylvan son lived for centuries, lending his brutal power in the endless war against the Feral Huntress and her spawn of curseblood. Known to have taken domain below the roots, in the sunken city of ancient Ajin’tatel. On the brink of being lost to madness and curse, of dark corruption, Nyralshirad was restored by his kin. If Fae, Shiok, and their slaves wish violence, so shall it be delivered them.
