Vaerek Rathkin

Wreathbound Ranger

“Vigar'am primori a sek. A sanguin, scal, a scal, mi'aghora. Lygos Amora, lygos Sareen, lygos Horlg, lygos Vana, lygos Mano. Sek Salaseth, te mi'aghora, vigar'am.”

— Vaerek's battle incantation

The bastard son of a Morathi noble, who chose self-exile over a life of luxurious derision. Vaerek left Tarn for the wilderness, then the military, then deserted in the Empire of Fala.

He lived as a woodland hermit in the Balamwood until the druids of Heartroot warmed to him. Archdruid Ulric kept the grove's criticism from becoming exile.

He befriended Saoirse and came to view her as his adoptive younger sister. When Ulric sent him to protect her after the Fading, he found her again among the Foundlings of Mora Mora.
