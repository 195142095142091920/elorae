Maegoroth

Prime Cosmic Lord

“Doom Bringer.”

— Maegoroth

(MAY-gorr-awth)

Prime Cosmic Lord

Highest Ancient said to have come from the Timeless Void to destroy the world. Creator of the Cosm, and shaper of planets, stars, and the void, where no time or form exists. Corruptor of the Dark Soul. Destroyed by Aghor during the calamitous Battle of the Dawn, so that the world would be saved.
