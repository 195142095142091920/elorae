Auralon

Ancient of Air and Sea

“Without Depth or Form.”

— Auralon

(AW-rah-lonn)

Ancient of Sky and Sea

Rogue Ancient said to have come from the Timeless Void to create the world. Creator of the winds and waters, and the Infinite. Destroyed by Maegoroth during the calamitous Battle of Dawn, as punishment for the Great Heresy.
