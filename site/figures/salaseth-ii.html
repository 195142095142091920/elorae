— Salaseth

(SAH-lah-seth)

Ancient of War

Spawn of the Rogue Ancients said to have been created to defend the world against annihilation. Father of the Archdragons. Destroyed by Maegoroth’s design during the calamitous Battle of the Dawn, so that the world would go unguarded.
