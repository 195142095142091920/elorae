  • The Weaver, King of Crystal, Drake of the Gem Waste, Gemdrake, Manadrake, The Sagedragon, Spinner of Threads

Dragonlord of the Kindred, born of sin. By his father’s command, was sent to dwell in the lands of Crystal, far to the south, to learn the powers of the gem wastes. A wizened sage who studies what he calls the Current, by which he provides illuminating counsel to the Kindred. As souls are threaded, woven through time, the weaver pulls upon them with great sorcery.
