Lore

“Winter’s dread beauty, borne of majestic rime, where wander ghosts among the frost”

— Aszurithice
  • Dragonqueen, The Frostwyrm, Winterdrake, Celurna’s Chosen

Dragonlord of the Kindred, born of sin. By her own will, chose to dwell in the lands of winter, to protect her home. It is said the frozen lands are filled with ghosts, the fallen of the old wars against the Hungerer. In the tundra, Celurna’s grace touched the living, and too she chose the daughter of Nathalor as her champion. But now, the hungering tribes grow in number and the ghosts of the fallen are restless. Aszurithice, in draconic form, brings icy death to those who have had their souls stolen.
