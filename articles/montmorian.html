Lore

“Immortal and pure, of a right exalted and kingly, lord of land divine”

— Montmorian
  • King of Fala, Heir-Prince of Nathalor, Dragon Prince, Kingdrake, The Mighty and Immortal Dragon

Dragonlord of the Kindred, born of sin. By his father’s command, was sent to dwell in the lands of Golos, to learn the ways of the lords of yore. Was a general of Fala in the twilight years of the fourth era, and was chosen for the throne following the loss of King Gadros at the turn of the age. A divine instrument of the Holy Three, by their protection was he and the people of his city saved.
