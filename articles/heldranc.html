Lore

“O mighty lord, arisen of stone and flame, betwixt cold and ash from ancient and grim legend”

— Heldranc
  • Father of Dragons, The Dragonlord, Dragon of the Old Blood, King of Nathalor

Great Lord of Dragons, ancient and wise, born before the Darkness first swept the land, in the second age. After the extermination of the dragons by the Dragonslayers of Raim, Heldranc fled into the underworld. Within the Starry Kingdom, he lived, allegiant to House Manos, even in its madness, until the House’s betrayal. Founded the kingdom of Nathalor, to save his kin. Within it, the weathered lord bides his time, preparing his Kindred for what is to come.
