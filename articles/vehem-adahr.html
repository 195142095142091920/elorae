Lore

“As prophesied, the Black Storm has come to pass, and we drink upon the power of our prize. Rejoice, for beyond the Auracine, an old king has listened, too.”

— Vehem
  • The Black Storm, The Dreaming King, The Deep Dreamer, The Black Dreamer, Nightmare Consort

Great demonic lord from the underworld. Said to have risen forth in the wake of the Grand Nightmare of Nereb. His fell reign began when he conquered the Deep Demesne of Uyin, joining the northern, southern, western and eastern kingdoms into a unified sovereignty known as the Four Pillars of Uyin. As the ashes presaged, after the Black Storm, a splinter of God bathes in the river of dreams.
