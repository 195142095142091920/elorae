Lore

“By the bountiful crimson moon shall the corruption be purged. Darkness shall never come to this Kingdom of God.”

— Mano
  • King of the Mount, Liege of Blood, The Red Tyrant, Blessed of the Moon

Great Lord of the Glaurfeldt, who protects the entirety of the region under the power of Cressana, the Crimson Moon. After the moon rose, casting its fell light across the land, the king and his knights scoured the realm of the corruption of his forsaken daughter, Filibeth. The scriptures spoke of an Elder God from another world, from which Mano’s purpose was discovered.
