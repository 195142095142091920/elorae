Act III, Chapter XLIV

They followed Aoife’s lead toward the withered tree and found it standing in a shallow lake, its roots gripping a gnarled bank at the water’s edge. Filibeth knelt by the lakeside to commune with it and attempt a purification. Saoirse joined her. The ground directly beneath Saoirse cleared, but the rest of the corruption would take far longer time than they had. Filibeth sank deeper into the working until she entered a trance.

Vaerek and Galand kept watch. Through his draconic sight Vaerek saw the tree’s core reaching outward in tendrils, and he warned the others to stay back. Galand shook Filibeth to wake her and was struck by a rush of flashing, dreamlike images. She fell onto her back and came to herself. Laughter rose in the distance. It unnerved Hanto and Kojin; they drew their blades and called the company to fight. The cursed of Olesh were upon them. The sky darkened and rain came quickly. On the tree a contorted humanoid face of twisted wood appeared, its eyes glowing red-orange.

The ghouls advanced through the muddy shallows. Hanto moved to meet them and told Baiji to shout if anything came on her side. She loosed an arrow tipped with bluish-white light. One of the things struck Hanto; he held. Galand’s first strikes failed to land. Silar rose on a swirl of embers and took the air, while Yuo watched from a heavy branch above. Kojin closed with the heavy sword and cut one of the undead down. Saoirse raised a wall of water from the river, catching several of the ghouls inside it and holding the rest at a distance. Vaerek drove Valeburn through the barrier in a short stab; steam rose where the blade met the water, though the blows went wide.

Telorin looked past the wall. The face in the bark remained, its eyes darting and the wood twisting. He cast forth a cloud of rusted air into the rear of the pack, then stepped back. Hanto struck again. Baiji fell back toward Kojin and put arrows into the things pressing him. Some of the ghouls forced their way through the water and came around Galand; one left Vaerek drained, though he shook the worst of it off.

Silar hovered above the clump and called for the others to clear away. Galand’s next efforts faltered. The company began sharing the labor of the remnants: Hanto’s blade, Silar’s fire, Baiji’s bow, Kojin’s sword. Saoirse cast a mercy meant to blunt the undead’s blows against the living, then moved clear. Baiji put arrows into the ghouls flanking Galand, one of them true. Telorin stepped behind one that had reached Galand, his staff becoming a rapier, and drove a spellstrike home that left the creature bleeding. Silar answered with fire from above. Most endured it; three took the burn and kept coming, smoldering and still laughing. A current from Saoirse swept one back toward the far side. Galand landed a heavy blow at last. Yuo loosed from the branch. Perstrin tested an icicle against the one Yuo had marked.

The face had not left the tree. The ghouls that remained were fewer, but they were not finished, and Saoirse had taken hard hits. Vaerek was already turning toward her as the rain continued and the laughter had not yet gone silent.
