Filibeth

(Fill-ih-BETH)

The Promised Daughter

The Waif, The Forsaken, Princess of the Glaurfeldt

Prodigious daughter of the Blood King and the Queen Sorceress of the Glaurfeldt, who as a child, found a Tree of God within the deep forest. In her adolescence, she crossed into the Darklands, where it is rumored she consorted with a servant of darkness. Upon the pink leaves, it is said stories are written - histories untold and yet to come - but now, the stories are not as they were.

"By my hand, never shall the sins of my father be felt by another. Through the leaves, my legacy shall be succor and serenity. The trees of God shall stand as hope against the Darkness."

Mano

(MAH-noh)

Lord of the Glaurfeldt

King of the Mount, Liege of Blood, The Red Tyrant, Blessed of the Moon

Great Lord of the Glaurfeldt, who protects the entirety of the region under the power of Cressana, the Crimson Moon. After the moon rose, casting its fell light across the land, the king and his knights scoured the realm of the corruption of his forsaken daughter, Filibeth. The scriptures spoke of an Elder God from another world, from which Mano's purpose was discovered.

"By the bountiful crimson moon shall the corruption be purged. Darkness shall never come to this Kingdom of God."
