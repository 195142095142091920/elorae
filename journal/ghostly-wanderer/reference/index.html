Private Journal

The Ghostly Wanderer

Time

  • The Eras. The history of Elorae is counted in eras and years. IV-470 means the 470th year of the Fourth Era.
  • The Burning of the K'bas (IV-470). The year which Mano and the dragon Heldranc set fire to the K'bas Forest, and everything changed.
  • The present. Some two thousand years later, in the Fifth Era. The present year is V-44.

People

  • Filibeth. Daughter of King Mano. As a child she found the pink tree in the K'bas and grew fond of it, then left in her teenage years. She returned one day, distraught, and never left the tree ever after. On the day of the Burning she vanished through a portal of green light, rescued by an extraordinarily powerful wizard and a party of outsiders you had never seen.
  • Mano. King of the Glaurfeldt, once famed as a devout and holy ruler and a defender against the dark. He burned the K'bas, cut down its tree and raised his axe against his own daughter. In the present, I know him to be stronger than ever, and he is known as the Liberator King, for he has crushed the demonic forces around him. Gamorth and Aborog both have fallen to his axe. Naerlaana cowers in the depths. The Dark is his final foe.
  • Cressana. Queen of the Glaurfeldt, Mano's wife and Filibeth's mother, a sorceress of old blood. By the time of the Burning she was dead. Her daughter would not speak of her, else she fall to tears. I learned that Mano killed her, sacrificing her sorcerous soul to raise into the sky, as an everlasting blood moon. It is said Mano's forces are blessed by this fell moonlight and are granted unnatural arcane power against the Dark.
  • Heldranc. Last of the dragons. He flew with Mano's banners and brought the fire that burned the K'bas. Through the whispers of the Dark, I learned that he was once virtuous, a victim of the Raim Empire. The unholy deal was made with Mano to foster a clutch of new dragon-eggs.
  • Perstrin. An elder wizard marked with burning tattoos. He stopped time at the roots of the falling tree and opened the green portal. He saved Filibeth, and was cleaved in twain for it.
  • The strangers in the ash. I saw a company of travelers in foreign clothes appear in the burning wood, among them a priestess calm as still water and a ranger who met the dragon's eyes. Filibeth left with them.
  • Sceeia Soultender. Filibeth's handmaiden, and a spirit guide of the forest shrine. She was murdered by Mano's forces at the Burning.

The Disciples of the Void
Five lords of the deep domains who follow the whispers of the Living Dark and serve the Dark Divinities.

  • Bel Harath. Lord of the Molnaran Inferno, a demon of shadowfire who is Warrior King of Molnar. He commands a great fiery legion of Ashborne who will soon rise to the surface and lay waste to the empires of man.
  • Ito Gangara. Queen of the Inner Depths, ruler below the leylines and highest of the Disciples. Lairs in the far north, beyond the Desert of Aborog and even farther, beyond the Silver Forest. Beyond, below the glacial expanse, she is burrowed. The spirits she sends to the surface steal souls to return to her, so through their memories she can unravel the whispers. She is said to be the one to bridge the portal to the Void when the time comes.
  • Mintas Fae. Lord of the Everdark, a Dark Spirit whose words etch themselves into roots. He resides in the far east, the Everdark is a place where the sky is but massive, gnarled roots. He poisons the Archwood above with his incantations. His work is said to uncover a grave threat, of which the Dark wishes to use.
  • Vehem Adahr. Lord of the Four Pillars, called the Black Storm and the Dreaming King, conqueror of Uyin. In the far west, beneath the land of God, the warlord Adahr gathers his forces. A lord of Nightmare, greatest champion of Nereb. It is said his forces suicide when their work is done, for he always replenishes his ranks.
  • Vorn. The Dark Prince of Enthar, above. Through the great bell, he calls pilgrims forth. He yearns to unlock the secrets of the Obliterated Mind. Through them, he seeks to re-form the Dark Soul. And deliver it to the Dark Divinities. Commands the Revenant Host, a dark army that assaults the surface world.


The Dark Divinities

  • Nul. God of Darkness. A being born of the Living Dark, pervading our world. By the great dark will, the shadow will prevail, and the Soul shall be reformed.
  • Nereb. God of Nightmare. Born of the world's fear and desolation after the death of God at the hands of the Raim Empire. By Nereb's will, nightmare and doom is cast over the land, searching through the dreams of humanity to harvest knowledge to re-form the Dark Soul.
  • Vana. Goddess of Death. Daughter of Amora, she rose to power in Yn'rylith, beside the great King Izil. By might, she ascended to divinity, and reaped the souls of her enemies. Now, as death incarnate, she culls the living as once was ordained by the Great One.

Places

  • The Glaurfeldt. Mano's holy kingdom in the under-realm.
  • The Starry Kingdom. The old name of Mano's realm, used only until the moon turned red.
  • The K'bas Forest. A misty wood in the center of the Glaurfeldt, your home, and home to the pink-leaved tree. It was burned to ash in IV-470.
  • The Oluine River. The river through the K'bas and across the eastern border.
  • The under-realm. The vast world beneath the surface, where the Glaurfeldt and the domains of the Dark both lie.
  • Enthar, the Darklands. Vorn's shadowed kingdom, where the Beckoning Bell tolls.
  • Molnar, the Everdark, Uyin, and the Inner Depths. The deep domains of the other Disciples.
  • Nathalor. The new home in the present year of Heldranc and his kin, known as the Dragonlords. It is in the far frozen north, under a sky where no sun rises.

Peoples and Powers

  • The Starfolk. People of the Glaurfeldt, also living in the K'bas, white-silver haired, paler of skin.
  • The Forestfolk. People of the K'bas, said to have been born of foreign origin. Features unlike common humans.
  • The Knights of Mano. Mano's soldiers, terrible beneath the Red Moon.
  • The Dark. The enemy that Mano's kingdom held back for generations.
  • The Living Dark. The muttering voice in the deep that Vorn and the Disciples follow. Throughout all shadow, and every dark place in the world, the Living Dark resides, and whispers can be heard. It is said that the Obliterated Mind of the Living Dark are the infinitesimal fragments of the Dark Soul.
  • The Dark Soul. The soul of the Ancient of Darkness, Sthistu. A soul of creation. It was once corrupted by Maegoroth, the Great One, to doom the world. Blasted to a trillion pieces by Salaseth, the Dragon God.
  • The Obliterated Mind. What the muttering voices are, all from one destroyed mind. The key to re-forming the Dark Soul.
  • The Light. The force that once was entwined with the Dark, in duality. Now, seeks to root out the servants of the whispers.
  • The Empire of Raim. The great and terrible empire led by the triune lords - Elraim, Malicator, and Rathalon. They conquered the entirety of Ilium Aghor and by some unknown manner, entered the realm of God and slew him, 470 years before the Burning of the K'bas.
  • Aghor. God. The old god of creation, who created Elorae. Enemy of Maegoroth. Killed by the Lords of Raim. Mano was a champion of Aghor.

Relics and Signs

  • The Godtree. A tree of pink leaves in the heart of the K'bas, a splinter of God, beneath which time ran slow. Mano cut it down. It is said the Godtree was formed when God was killed by the empire of Raim.
  • Glaidrogh. The ancestral great-axe of Mano's house. It felled the mystical tree in one stroke. It is enchanted with immense power.
  • The Red Moon. The moon that rose red over the Glaurfeldt and never paled. From that night Mano's knights grew terrible. Also known as Cressana, the red moon. A bloody symbol of unwilling sacrifice, ever present in the sky.
  • The Beckoning Bell. The bell of Enthar, whose toll draws pilgrims toward the Dark.
