Private Journal

The Ghostly Wanderer

Heldranc's Tale


"Allow me to tell a story, born of fire. Allow the flames of my words to illuminate thine minds. To give understanding.

From the beginning, I shall start, and honesty I vow.

I was born long ago. A wyrmling was I, in the second age of this world. My brood of the mountains of Shasir lorded and lived, for a time, until the wars came.

When the kings warred against those invaders, known as the Val'norain.

When the cycle began.

Throughout the Dark Age, I lived. Until a commander called upon my kin to fight against the Dark. In those wars we fought, and lost, but in time, saw victory, and the age turned once the dark was felled.

Then came the Third Age, which saw the rise of the Empire of Raim. A small lordling, ambitious, in the north of the Godland - the land you call home. Golos, was controlled by the Golossian Lords. This ambitious king, the lordling, helped his people. He went against the Golossian Lords in combat, until the Long Battle of Nilheen, which drew to a stalemate. After a fortnight, they joined their banners. The Lord-Commander of the Alliance of Golos, Malicator - and this young lordling, the white-haired Elraim. From there, they journeyed south, conquering all under their banner of might, until they came to Aghoras, the Kingdom of Sorcery, which no longer stands. They knew they could not take Aghoras and its mage-knights on the battlefield. All thought Elraim's conquest would be halted, but it was not. Much to the dismay of their people, the King-Mage of Aghoras, Rathalon, joined the two lords - becoming three. And so they went, ever southward, uniting all of Ilium Aghor, under the triple-fist of the Empire. The first empire to control the entire continent.

The Lords of the Raim Empire.

And so, eastward across the sea they went, to continue their conquering. This Empire of Raim would rise to heights unseen.

Until, they were corrupted. As so I believe.

For there is no other explanation for their actions.

Their assault on the Godrealm.

Through some cursed and forbidden power, Elraim and Malicator and Rathalon devised a plan to pierce the veil between our world and the Realm Above, and slay God, Aghor.

Our lord.

In his own realm.

And so they did. The impossible occurred. They plundered the Tripartite Soul, split amongst them. And with their power they lorded, over a godless realm.

The preceding events of the Empire, I will not delve into. But in their power, the wretched King, Elraim, created an order of elite dragonslayers. The Dragonslayers of Raim hunted and killed every dragon, down to the very last.

I watched them butcher my kin.

Casting our bones into flame, turning them to dust. Scattering the ash to the wind, to remove every trace of dragonkind.

They removed every last defense this world has. The rightful defenders of the lands, against the darkness.

A stature granted to us, by God himself. They knew that this protection must be destroyed. For I know the darkness within Elraim. Though I never saw him, I know of his malice, his corruption. It was absolute.

And so I fled. I fled, far below - toward the Nexus of the Leylines - into the lands you know as the Glaurfeldt. There I would find the Starry Kingdom.

The Kingdom of the Stars, ruled by the great Manosan Lords.

There, I would find House Manos.

They exalted my form. Gave me protection. Unburdened by Elraim's vision, of his cleansing. And so my loyalty to House Manos was then sealed. I lived there, with them, for an age. But... darkness crept in around us. The Demonic Lords and their taint, their own corruption, encroached on all sides.

I watched Mano grow, as a boy, as I watched his father before him. Kings of legend, respected by their people. Until, his madness destroyed everything. His great wife, the mythic sorceress Cressana, was sacrificed - murdered - for her power. To bolster his army.

Cressana is sacrificed by the Circle of Sorcery.

And her daughter, Filibeth, imprisoned, shackled.

His madness drew him to the wood. But he could never breach it. At this time, I grew distant from Mano. For many years, decades, I lived within the cavern of the broodlord. There the bones of an old dragon - perhaps one of the first. Her fossilized remains, stood silent watch over hundreds of petrified eggs. Mano wished of my assistance, but I declined. Once Cressana rose to the sky, her soul tormented in stasis, I wanted nothing of the company of Mano.

But I saw before me the future of my race, the future of this worlds' protection. The future of this world, petrified.

And so an unholy deal was brokered.

Through Cressana, and the power of the Circle of Sorcery - they would give life to the eggs. And I would help him reach the center of the forest.

And so, we met on that day. My flames burned thine forest to the ground. We slaughtered thine people. And with Glaidrogh, he cleaved that tree asunder.

Heldranc's final night in the Glaurfeldt.

After this, I took flight, returning to the eggs. Wanting of solitude, and to wash the ever-stain of blood from my hands. But in time, Mano would come to me, demanding the eggs serve him. So he may conquer outward, to slay the demonic lords. To take the lands under his rule. And so, in my gullet, I swallowed the eggs - as many as could. And I flew, out of the Glaurfeldt, far to the North, to the Winterlands, where there is no sun. Where then the eggs would hatch, and our kindred would form.

Wyrmlings would become dragons. And through sorcery, we hid ourselves as humans, in exile, to survive. Over the aeons, we thought it necessary to spread across the lands, to safeguard the trees. For they are the last vestige of God. They must be protected against the darkness, for they are under assault.

Thus, we are here in this grave place, Nathalor, our home. "

Heldranc, Last of the Old Dragonsblood
