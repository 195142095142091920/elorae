Nereb

(Nehr-EBB, or Nurr-EBB)

God of Dark Dreams, Nightmares, and Fear

The Dark Dreamer, Nightmare Lord

Dreadful lord of horror and nightmare. Manifested after the death of God, when the world fell silent. Dwells deep below, by the rivers of mana where the carcasses of the old ones rot. By his dark dreams, nightmares are woven into the minds of mortals. Their invaded dreams grant knowledge of the mysteries of the Obliterated Mind.

Nul

(NULL, or NOOL)

God of Darkness and Shadow

The Dark One

Said to be born of the Living Dark, a spawn of shadow incarnate. Solitary and silent god with a darkened history. Worshiped by practitioners of the Dark arts, thieves, and assassins. Sacred texts of the Dark One portent a time when the suns are never to rise again. Forbidden deity to Fena’s faithful. Nul embodies the Living Dark. His formation guides pilgrims to deliver unto him the whispers of the Obliterated Mind.

Vana

(VAH-nuh)

Goddess of Death

Queen of Death

Daughter of Amora, Goddess of Life, who against which, an eternal war is waged. Diametrically rejected the ways of her mother and descended into the world below to seek the ways of the Ashen Lords, but instead found something greater. In their domain, thus she became a sorcerous queen and ruled by might. Greater then, ascended to divinity, she found the scriptures of the old Dark, of the Second Age. Their Knights, their mission, their purpose - to bring forth the Great One. Though he is no more, in the same manner shall she bring forth the reformation of the Dark Soul.




The Disciples of the Void

Enshrouded in darkness and gloom, the five disciples of the unconquerable void gather. In their deep domains, the five lords follow the Whispers of the Obliterated Mind - the Living Dark. By the secrets spoken by the shadow, the disciples and their forces seek to reconstruct the Dark Soul, and deliver it to the Dark Divinities.

Bel Harath

(BELL ha-RATH)

Lord of the Molnaran Inferno

Darkfire Lord, Liege of the Darkened Flame, Warrior King of Molnar, Demon of the Gorge, Shadowfire Demon, Infernal Lord

Great demonic lord from the underworld. Lord of Molnar, the volcanic depths, where the darkened fires burn eternally. Set on a convergence point of a great leyline, an overflowing mana river fills the infernal kingdom of the Molnaran horde with colossal strength. In their magmatic domain, the flame has married the shadow and turned black, casting fell light. The shadowfires rage with dark ferocity and seek to feed on the silent souls of the peaceful. As the souls feed the flames, so too do they feed Bel Harath. By his unholy rage shall the Dark Divinity be made whole.

"By the darkened flame, the Molnaran horde shall pulse with an intensity unseen. Across the realms, we rise. Let the black legions pour forth, for our destiny comes."

Ito Gangara

(EE-toh Gonn-GARR-uh)

Queen of the Inner Depths

Voidspawn Queen, The Hungerer, The Deepest One, Queen of the Core

Great demonic queen from the underworld and highest disciple. Ruler of the inner depths below the leylines, where the void seeps in. From the deepest shadow, in the deepest core, Gangara basks in horrific ecstasy as she feeds on abyssal energy. With her uninhibited power, she casts souls forth - sentient parasitic larva - to leech upon the souls of mortals. All souls she consumes, she reanimates into eternal submission - for her own pleasure and for the reconstruction of the Dark. By a hivemind of forbidden memories, the darkness of Gangara will permeate the realm.

"All are one within the core. The everlasting, complete Dark was denied victory once before. Now it lives, and in Divinity shall it be made whole."

Mintas Fae

(MINN-tahss FAY)

Lord of the Everdark

Dark Spirit, Everdark King, Gloomlord

Great demonic lord from the underworld. Lord of the Everdark, below the green domain, where the shadows linger like fog. When the Black Lord came from the west during the Gloom, from beyond the inferno, the Dark Spirit arose to listen. Now, the sylvan groves rot and wither, shadows pool in deep places, and the umbral words etch themselves into the roots. The words of Fae are translations from the darkness. Thus, is spoken, after the transformation comes to pass, the night shall come, and by the grace of the moon shall we ascend.

"Gods' splinter soaks in the river of dreams. Uyin has fallen, Uyin has risen. As shall we. Whilst the Red Moon shall rise, to bless us all."

Vehem Adahr

(veh-HEM uh-DARR)

Lord of the Four Pillars

King of Uyin, The Black Storm, The Dreaming King, The Deep Dreamer, The Black Dreamer, Nightmare Consort

Great demonic lord from the underworld. Said to have risen forth in the wake of the Grand Nightmare of Nereb. His fell reign began when he conquered the Deep Demesne of Uyin, joining the northern, southern, western and eastern kingdoms into a unified sovereignty known as the Four Pillars of Uyin. Those slain by the storm arise as minions, imbued with the undying dark sorcery. As the ashes presaged, after the Black Storm, a splinter of God bathes in the river of dreams.

"As prophesied, the Black Storm has come to pass, and we drink upon the power of our prize. Rejoice, for beyond the Auracine, an old king has listened, too."

Vorn

(VORN)

Lord of the Darklands of Enthar

The Dark Prince, Lord of Shadow, Prince of Darkness, The Black

When the shadow crossed the lands, the royals of Enthar recoiled and hid in their fortresses of stone. The old Darkness came, and through it, Nul ascended to divinity. In his umbral path, the young prince Vorn followed, unafraid. The Dark Prince of Enthar proclaims to speak with what he calls the Living Dark, for it is said muttering voices can be heard from within. Now, petitioners from across the enshadowed country and beyond journey as pilgrims to tell the Lord the secrets they hear, wishing to join his growing congregation. Though he seems as a man, nothing remains of his mortal shell but an aphotic likeness.

"One must prostrate utter devotion if thou wishest to hear the unspoken utterances of the Dark. If thy devotion is not absolute, thou shalt hear naught but the gnashing of teeth."
