Demonic Lords of the Fourth Era

Those foul lords who encroach upon the holiness of the Glaurfeldt, from whom Mano protects his people.

Aborog

(Ah-BORR-ogg)

Liege of the White Sands of Norr

The Demon of Old, Wurmlord, Demiliege, The Burrowed One

In the shining white sands of the northern Desert of Norr, Aborog slithers and burrows. It is said the full form of the Wurmlord has never been seen in full - some say it coils endlessly beneath the dunes. Isolated in the wasteland, the ancient city of Abor'rylith still stands, where the ashes of old still remain and the embers of vengeance never fade. Ancient occult myth says that the desert was once an ocean, where deepfolk dwelled in island villages, but when the Spawn Wars began, all was lost.

"White sand under the black sky, where once was a sapphire ocean. To the abyss with the Wurmlord, for he brought forth the apocalypse after the Withering."



Gamorth

(Gah-MORRTH)

King of Dathenor

Lord of the Ill Rains, King of the Wetlands, Wretch Baron

The ill rains of Dathenor fall as a warning, west of the Glaurfeldt, before the endless Auracine. King Gamorth and his vassal lords rule the wet lands with corrupt oppression. His gluttonous and vile figure is known to many, as ailing villagers pledge fealty before his rotten throne with tithe and sacrifice. His concubines, known as the wretch queens, are offered to him during the debaucherous Festival of the Father. In the lands of Dathenor, those who till the fields pray for mercy, and pay with their blood, but the rains wash all away.

"Baron, O baron, grant us mercy. For all we have given, grant us mercy."



Haethlin

(HAYTH-linn)

King of the Silent City

The White, The Silent King, Demon of the Light, King Diviner

In the east, beyond the Glaurfeldt and across the Sunken Fields, dwells the Silent City and its ponderous ruler, Haethlin the White. A city of sages and prognosticators that ever portent the coming days, gazing into their enchanted reflective pools. In exchange for their prophetic foresight, the Silent City remains unmolested by the ills of the Spawn Wars. Haethlin himself is said to be an old demonic lord, a vigorous soul from before the enslavement of the Deepfolk. Though greatly powerful in his divination, Haethlin seeks ever more to illuminate the shadow - to bring silent certainty to the cold, uncertain darkness. The lord is said to have gained his power from a being of pure light, when it long ago sought apostles to aid its pursuit of the Living Dark.

"As his charge, the White King's illumined order quiets the whispers. The holy light of divination shall carry us to lucidity."



Naerlaana

(Nair-LAH-NAH)

Queen of the Auracine

Abyss Queen, Serpent Queen, Mother of the Abyssal Depths, Fathom Demoness

In the endless Auracine, the inverted rains ever rise, past the wetlands of Dathenor. Travelers have tried to cross the abyssal expanse, for it is said beyond lies the land of the Elder Gods. Naerlaana brings ruin to those that cross the waters, and many legend there to be a bottomless depth, filled with all manner of cruel monstrosities who serve her with inerrant loyalty. Across her domain, there lies dotted islandry with inhabitants much like those of the land-kingdoms, though the flesh of the drowned feed the demoness and her spawn.

"The deeps of her domain are death. The lands of the Elder Gods are never meant to be walked by mortal souls."



Vorn

(VORN)

Lord of the Darklands of Enthar

The Dark Prince, Lord of Shadow, Prince of Darkness, The Black

When the shadow crossed the lands, the royals of Enthar recoiled and hid in their fortresses of stone. The old Darkness came, and through it, Nul ascended to divinity. In his umbral path, the young prince Vorn followed, unafraid. The Dark Prince of Enthar proclaims to speak with what he calls the Living Dark, for it is said muttering voices can be heard from within. Now, petitioners from across the enshadowed country and beyond journey as pilgrims to tell the Lord the secrets they hear, wishing to join his growing congregation. Though he seems as a man, nothing remains of his mortal shell but an aphotic likeness.

"One must prostrate utter devotion if thou wishest to hear the unspoken utterances of the Dark. If thy devotion is not absolute, thou shalt hear naught but the gnashing of teeth."



Yglaristh

(EE-GLAR-issth)

Ruler of the Silver Forest

The Wailing Death, Spectre Demon, Silver Demon, Tormentor

In the northland, the white sands of Norr meet the trees of the Silver Forest, which some rumor has no end. The silver-leaved expanse is ruled by the Spectre King, who knows not rest. The Silver Forest is spoken of in myth, as a place of eternal punishment for those who have committed grievances worthy of exile. Many of these exiles arrive from the south - if they survived the wastes. The haunting wails of Yglaristh torment those in his domain, and in their madness and broken will, join his ever-growing covenant of servants. Those who serve are returned to the lands from whence they came, sent abroad to hail his mysterious bidding.

"The enchantment is unbreakable. None of the mystics could break the hex. His soul is taken, and something possesses his body as a vessel. With silvered pupils he stares, ever since he returned."
