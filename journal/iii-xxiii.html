Act III, Chapter XXIII

The Kindred entered the grand marble throne room of Queen Primwyn, flanked by royal guards. The chamber was a display of wealth and power: high ceilings, masterwork sconces, and heavy red tapestries framed the upholstered stone throne. The queen sat in regal attire, her foreign-born attendant standing close at hand. Foggy daylight poured through tall windows that overlooked the city and bay.

The queen greeted them formally, already briefed on their names, origins, and claims. She cut straight to the matter: they had spoken of a cure for the Red Scourge. Telorin clarified that they offered no ready-made remedy for the afflicted, but rather a path to stop the source itself - believing that ending the origin might free those already cursed. Saoirse and the others expanded: they sought Darcdressil, the Palemoon King, whom they believed still lived within the Ochre Depths, near the root of the curse. They named the Six Kings as the likely architects of the Scourge, spreading it through dreams. Vaerek added that his own draconic vigor agitated the afflicted, offering another thread of connection.

When the queen pressed on how such ancient figures could still endure, the party spoke carefully of long-lived lords and powerful blood. Saoirse eventually revealed the Godtree leaf, linking it to Sar Sareen’s scriptures and the tree that once served as a conduit of divine power. She argued that with the leaf, draconic strength, and an army’s support, a path into the Depths might finally be forced open - allowing Yollonde to liberate its people as Sar Sareen once had.

Queen Primwyn listened with growing gravity. She confirmed much of what they suspected: the Six Kings still lingered in the wasteland, deformed and undying; Darcdressil, known to her realm as the dracoonic 'Terror of the Depths', fought them endlessly within the dunes; and the Scourge spread through cursed dreams. Yet she refused any call to raise banners. Marching an army a month’s journey into the Ochre Depths would leave her kingdom undefended and cost countless lives. She preferred to focus on developing elixirs that induced dreamless sleep, ensuring her people relief.

The conversation turned sharper when the party revealed their communion with living dragons -King Montmorian of Fala among them - and their mission to reunite the scattered Dragon Lords. Rallice knelt and testified that he had seen his own king alive and allied with the Lords. Vaerek displayed his draconic blood through his growing primal form. The grand revelation stirred the Queen as she leaned forward with great worry and shock. Saoirse spoke with passion of hope, renewal, and second chances, drawing parallels between Arleth’s defiance of a predatory world and the Kindred’s belief that the dragons could yet choose a better path. The queen remained unmoved. History, she said, showed only that dragons brought scorching war and scarred lands. She would not ally her people with such power, no matter the sincerity of the travelers before her. She wished them victory against the Scourge, but dismissed them as fools for serving dragons.

Outside the castle, the party processed the outcome. They had confirmed Darcdressil’s identity and location, learned the true scale of the threat, and gained allies in Greymoor, yet they lacked the force to press further. Telorin and others argued that continuing alone would be suicide; better to return to the Enclave, seek the remaining Dragonlords, and build strength before any assault on the Depths. Perstrin commented on the extraordinarily peculiar absence of magic within the throne room. Brigh and Jiatra agreed the queen’s caution was sound from a ruler’s perspective. Warm farewells were exchanged. The hunters of Greymoor departed to seek apothecaries and dream-potions, while the Kindred sought a quiet alley to contact Filibeth.

The leaf glowed. Filibeth answered and drew them home through the familiar sensation of warmth and weightlessness. They reappeared in the sanctuary of basalt and stone, greeted by Filibeth and the presence of Heldranc. Montmorian soon joined them. The party delivered their report: the queen’s refusal, the confirmation that Darcdressil remained trapped and possibly corrupted within the Ochre Depths, the Six Kings’ active use of a Godtree to spread the Scourge through dreams, and the necessity of greater force. The Dragonlords were not pleased with the reveal of their existence, though some viewed it as inevitable.

Heldranc and Montmorian absorbed the news grimly. The Six Kings still lived; Darcdressil would never be safe alone. War would eventually be required, but first the remaining kin must be found. The next destination was set: The Winterlands, where Aszurithice was last known to aid the Champions of Celurna against the shamans of Gangar. Preparations for the frigid journey would begin in short time. Wren’s departure was noted with quiet concern. Private conversations followed - while the rest of the Kindred rested, the weight of the failed audience and the long road ahead settled over them.
