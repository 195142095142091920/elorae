Act III, Chapter XXIV

Morning in the sanctuary was brief and the Kindred awoke readied for their next quest. The Six Kings had seen them in Syr Sable; whatever watched from the shadows might already be moving. They gathered once more to the chamber of the lords, stocked, geared, and clad in cold-weather armor prepared by the Lords themselves.

Pethengorom stood forward as the others drew aside. He had touched the Current that morning and found their sister. Aszurithice lived. Her soul still burned with the same fierce light. He could not swear to her safety, only that she still fought with her own will. The cause of her disappearance remained unknown.

The Lords would not travel with them. Pethengorom and Nyralshirad had business of their own in Aelyn, the land which Vabaldinur, the Warden once guarded. The travelers' path lay elsewhere.

Pethengorom opened the portal. Green magical light flared and a blast of absolute cold tore into the chamber. The Lords bowed their heads in farewell. Vaerek met Heldranc’s eyes one last time, offered Filibeth a quiet word, and stepped through.

They emerged into Frostfell Reach.

Wind howled across a world of white. Snow came to the knees with every step. The suns were a dim rumor behind an unending icy storm. Saoirse left Aoifa behind in the sanctuary; the hawk would not have survived. Lanterns replaced torches. Visibility shrank to almost nothing. For nearly two hours they trudged north-northwest through the blizzard, following the faint pull of Aszurithice’s presence.

Then the whispers began - thin, unintelligible voices carried on the wind. Moments later the snow itself rose into tall spectral forms wielding ghostly blades, their eyes piercing blue-light orbs.

Combat was immediate and brutal.

The ghosts struck in wide, sweeping emanations. Steel passed through them as though they were mist. Only magic and spirit-touched force seemed to bite. Vaerek’s dragonfire carved brief tunnels of light through the storm. Telorin’s spellstrikes and Saoirse’s verdant rays found purchase. Galand’s greathammer, when his eyes began to glow with that strange divine spark, connected as though the spirits were flesh. He and Rallice struggled in their heavy armor already rimed with frost.

A spectral sorcerer lingered somewhere in the white, casting suggestions that forced Telorin to extinguish his own lights and later isolated him in a private hallucination of solitude. Wave after wave pressed in. Perstrin cast a spell of flight, to escape the melee. Rallice fell to the ground. Saoirse poured healing from her staff and made hasty prayers to Arleth. The party was driven to the edge of collapse.

Then the wind itself changed.

A vast, freezing gust swept the ridge. The blizzard parted. From the newly cleared air came a piercing, bone-deep shriek that echoed across the mountains. The remaining ghosts turned from the party as if in retreat. A colossal shape of pure frost and scale emerged upon the cliff edge. Absolute cold poured from its open maw, a luminous blue beam which rimed the entire mountainside in crystalline ice. But... the blessing of the Kindred held, as it did in Athag'ir. The Kindred felt the cold but were not harmed.

The dragon’s form flew upward, then descended further down the pass. In a great vortex of swirling snow, the form collapsed and disappeared. Where it had stood then stood a woman in armor of living ice, black hair streaming in the wind, and eyes the piercing blue of glacial depths.

She looked upon Vaerek, then the rest, then spoke.

“Pleasure to meet thee, Vaerek Rathkin. I have heard of thine endeavors.”

Her voice carried easily across the storm. Through the lingering magical distortion that still clouded Telorin’s senses, the words also found him, as though she addressed him instead of the Drakewarden.

“My kin have called upon me, but I have not heeded their call. For I fight against Ito Gangara. My presence here is needed.”

Behind her, the last of the spectral forms dissolved into nothing. The party stood in the sudden stillness, breath steaming, weapons still raised. Rallice lay motionless in the snow where he had fallen for the final time. Galand rolled him onto his back, and gazed upon his eyes, destroyed by some manner of killing magic. The life had already fled from him despite every desperate heal Saoirse could pour into his body. She shouted his name, they held him to awaken, but, the Falan knight was gone.
