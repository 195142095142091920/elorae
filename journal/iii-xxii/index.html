Act III, Chapter XXII

The party departed Greymoor with Sir Brigh and Lady Jiatra as guides and holy representatives. The road to Yollonde City stretched nearly a moon's cycle beneath ceaseless drizzle. Red Scourge encounters thinned nearer populated lands, yet each clash carried grim weight. Vaerek tested his draconic vigor against the afflicted, noting their agitation to his healing flames. Galand's divine spark flared in battle, echoing the crimson dreams that lingered. Brigh shared fireside lore of Sar Sareen, while Jiatra's prayers warded the night's chill. Wren's absence weighed heavily; distant lute songs in inns only sharpening the memory of his sudden departure.

At the bay, they boarded small sailboats for the final crossing. The island capital emerged from the mist—stone spires, lantern-lit harbors bustling with merchants and royal guards. The rain never ceased, but the sight of ordered civilization brought weary relief. Customs passed smoothly, aided by the mayor's sealed letter and the hunters' reputation. They made straight for the Hall of Diplomats, presenting their case for urgent audience regarding the Red Scourge. They displayed their sincerity through piety and foreign connection to the Mighty and Immortal Fala.

Before their formal audience, the group sought respite in a tavern. In a secluded booth, conversation turned candid and unguarded. The travelers explained their journey and origins to the priestess and paladin. Saoirse revealed the enchanted Godtree leaf, presenting it to Jiatra as a sign of good faith, a real symbol matching the legends of Sareen. Within minutes, a presence awakened, and whispers filled the air - chanting voices invoking the Six Kings. "Amirian. Tath. Imentos. Valinvoren. Hetraxis. Kaliant." The leaf glowed with immense power, resisting movement or dispel. Raw infinity surged, threatening to teleport them as they had been before. Galand slumped unconscious. Vaerek, intervening with his vigor, suffered a blackout - his eyes glowing red before fading, leaving him dazed with a splitting headache.

Chaos gripped the booth. Perstrin channeled his mimicry, absorbing and stabilizing the wild magic as Telorin and Saoirse layered dispels and blessings. Brigh and Jiatra prayed fervently. Telorin focused on the voices and questioned them. The voices spoke of eternal power and the Palemoon, hinting at Darcdressil. Through desperate communion, Saoirse reached Filibeth through the leaf once more. The princess's guidance anchored them, her voice cutting through the storm and providing stability. The surge subsided without displacing them, for the first time. Now it was seen, the leaf could channel both peril and salvation.

Shaken, the Kindred reflected. Galand awoke. Perstrin realized his tattoos had absorbed the leaf's power, granting him the ability to mimic and wield such immense magic in the future. The encounter forged deeper unity and underscored the Godtree's perilous centrality. Saoirse offered protective blessings as they retired and the tavern's warmth provided sanctuary against the constant rain.

The next morning, they returned to the Hall of Diplomats. With approved documentation in hand, they were escorted through the upper districts to the royal castle. The queen's handmaiden received them, confirming their credentials as Falan dignitaries and Scourgeslayers. They entered the grand receiving hall, where Queen Primwyn awaited on her throne.
