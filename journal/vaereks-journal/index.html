Act III, Chapter XXV, Communion

The steward led Vaerek upward through endless corridors and heavy stone doors. Each step felt longer than the last in the darkness of his blindness. At last the final threshold opened, and a blast of winter wind struck him full in the face. The howling of the open air filled the vast chamber beyond. The steward withdrew. The doors sealed shut behind him.

Heldranc’s voice came out of the cold.

He already knew of the blindness. Aszurithice had told him what she had learned in the deep places. The affliction was of the Void - Elder Magic, older and more poisonous than anything born of the Infinite. No mortal healing, not Saoirse's, could touch it.

Vaerek offered what remained of himself. He would stay behind. He would put down the sword and serve only as counsel to those who could still fight. He strode slowly towards the Lord.

Heldranc did not accept the surrender. Instead, he spoke of purpose.

Long ago Salaseth, Ancient of War, had been made to guard the world against the first great evil - the God of the Void, Maegoroth. The Doom Bringer. The dragons were forged to withstand that power and to cleanse it with fire. The darkness was only its tool. The Void itself was the true enemy. That charge had never ended, and it now fell to those who still carried the blood.

“Thou art of dragon’s blood,” Heldranc said. “Thou holdeth power within thyself beyond mortals.”

He stepped close and laid a heavy hand upon Vaerek's shoulder. Fire answered.

Not the wild, uncontrolled surge of Athag'ir, but a steady, rising heat that filled Vaerek from within. His eyes ignited. Sight returned - not ordinary vision, but something clearer and fiercer, of the Infinite itself. He saw Heldranc standing before him on the cliff’s edge, winter landscape falling away into the mist beyond, the blue cloak of Rallice he wore, whipping in the wind.

“Thy service as a blade shall not end here,” Heldranc said. “This is but a new beginning - for thyself as a vessel of the Kindred, and for the Kindred itself.”

The remaining two kin must be found. The forces of darkness already knew of their presence. No longer would the Lords hide in stone while their servants fell. War had come. It was time to draw swords and bear fangs.

Heldranc stepped backward off the ledge and fell into the white mist.

A moment later the mountain shook with a draconic cry that seemed to split the sky. From the snow erupted Heldranc’s true form - vast beyond measure, scales like living stone and obsidian, a core of fire glowing in his chest. For the first time in two thousand years the Last Dragon of the Old Blood flew.

He returned to the mountain cavern with crashing force. The cave shook. Heldranc took rumbling steps toward Vaerek, then lowered his massive head. Without words the command was clear. Vaerek climbed onto his back, securing himself among the boulder-like spines.

They launched into the freezing sky.

For a long while Heldranc carried him over the Winterlands, the first true flight of the Kindred’s founder since the long exile began. The world looked small from that height - ridges and valleys reduced to lines, the horizon endless. Vaerek felt both the terror and the deep rightness of it: the violent grandeur of the dragon, and the answering fire in his own blood.

When the flight ended, Vaerek dismounted. He watched as the Dragonlord began heaving great breaths. Dragonfire was coming. The same that burned the K'bas to cinders. With a great bellow, Heldranc breathed red-orange draconic flame upon the warden. Heldranc left him changed.

Vaerek immediately realized his hearing and smell now reached far beyond mortal limits. A permanent draconic sight remained - fiery, incomplete in detail, yet enough.

He returned to the lower halls with restored vision and a purpose renewed. The first place he went was to his priestess.
