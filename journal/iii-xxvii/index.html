Act III, Chapter XXVII

In Akath, the travelers took shelter in ancient ruins while the unnatural storm raged. Lightning blasted the wet dunes without pause. Pethengorom raised his protective prism and then left them, climbing a ruined tower. In a flash of lightning and a draconic cry he took true form. His wings and back were pure living crystal, refracting the storm and casting reflections of the ceaseless lightning. He flew north to survey the desert and search the current.

While they waited, Montmorian spoke of Fala. He had arrived after the fall of the Eren Dominion, rose through the ranks - known as the 'Gifted Knight' whose youth never faded - and been crowned when King Gadros was lost. He embraced the ideals of the Holy Three, and over the next forty years, built the greatest empire the world had known. Then, it was taken from him in a single day. All could see on his face the undeniable trauma that lingered. The power that destroyed it, he said, was beyond all power that he knew.

Pethengorom returned at dusk. The desert was occupied with a marching army - black-armored figures moving in disciplined squads, souls dim and wrong. When he made himself known, the lightning struck harder as if the storm itself answered. The host was heading north. They would wait in the ruins until Pethengorom gave them further instruction.

A week passed under constant thunder. Vaerek and Saoirse explored the ruins and found faded paint that once might have belonged to Fena. On the ninth day the host turned into a canyon that marked the border of the Celestial Sands. Pethengorom teleported the party to the ridge above them.

Below, the army marched under a pocket of clear air while the storm poured everywhere else. A cave mouth waited in their path. It was deemed likely that the host sought Cadarinost. If the host entered that cavern, draconic power would be useless against them. Montmorian and the party would go in first. Pethengorom would remain outside and deal with the army.

They ran. Galand toppled stone into the canyon. Saoirse raised vines and a wall of wind across the entrance. Pethengorom’s shriek split the sky; his magical fire spewed forth and rolled down the canyon and the host burned. The survivors who tried to reach the cave found only barriers.

Inside, the cavern grew colder and quieter. Stairs descended into worked stone. Campfires burned ahead. Vaerek’s Draconic Sight showed two living souls beside each fire - souls that ordinary eyes could not see. Saoirse approached with open hands and the calm of Arleth. One figure stepped out of invisibility, then others. Through Perstrin’s translation they named themselves the Phantoms, servants of Cadarinost.

The Revenant Host had plagued them for generations, but only in the last year had its numbers swollen from dozens to hundreds. The host stole their children with black magic; the faces returned years later empty of recognition. Cadarinost had granted them the gift of invisibility and charged them with defending the southern blooms of the Celestial Sands. He himself had gone north to those blooms a month ago and never returned.

The host that had marched on the canyon was now dead or dying. A Phantom woman named Taleera offered a cautious welcome. Outside, the storm still raged. Ahead, the blooms waited, and with them, Cadarinost.
