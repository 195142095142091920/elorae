Act III, Chapter XLV

The rain continued as Saoirse opened a circle of healing that restored the living and forced the laughter back from the nearest of the cursed. Filibeth stayed near the center of the company, watching every direction. Vaerek, hearing that Saoirse had been hurt, cut one of the ghouls in half with a pillar of flame and moved to reach her, though the attempt to heal her failed in his haste. He told the others to keep watch on Filibeth while Telorin and Silar pressed the remaining undead, and Yuo put a heavy arrow into one from a branch above.

Under the water a new sound rose, and a massively bloated, waterlogged zombie with bright orange eyes and a wide smile rose from the shallows. Perstrin slowed it from the air. Saoirse lashed another of the cursed and spoke of the things that did not belong in Arleth’s garden. Filibeth remained fixed on the face in the tree. Through his sight Vaerek saw the tendrils of its core reaching toward her, and she asked why, already dazed.

Vaerek sealed the last of his own wounds, called his wings, and took Filibeth into the air with him, shaking her and telling her to snap out of it and cleanse the tree. She looked at him through wet hair and asked why again. Telorin pinned the bloated one through the leg with a rising spike of metal. Galand erupted with laughter that filled the field while his own wings flaring as he rose to meet the bloated zombie’s eyes. The smaller cursed that had fallen began to rise again - missing limbs, one of them only a torso - and their laughter came all at once.

Baiji said they needed to leave or to burn the tree. Galand forced a moment of clarity and remained able to act, though the laughter still sat on him. Vaerek shifted Filibeth to one arm, hurled Valeburn into the bloated zombie so that it burst into flame and returned to his hand, and called to Yuo to ready a place in the tree. Telorin tripped the bloated one with sorcery. The face in the bark lost its smile and became a grimace; the laughter turned darker.

Saoirse judged the field and called that the undead would not stop rising until the tree was cleansed. Filibeth, still not fully present, said she thought she should see the face. Vaerek answered with Heldranc’s Presence, trying to drive out whatever held her. She spoke of dragons and of the tree, and asked why his eyes were as they were. He brought her toward Saoirse and told her to get the leaf so that Filibeth could be cleansed first.

Telorin dropped in green flame among a cluster of the cursed and called fiery wrath across them. More of the ghouls tried the wall of water. Galand put his head down and forced the tendrils that had been reaching into him to cut off; through soul sight Vaerek watched the knight’s soul harden into a denser core edged with a barrier. The face in the tree remained, grimacing, and the laughter had not fully left the field. Filibeth was still not fully present. The cursed kept rising. The company held the ground they had taken, but the tree had not been cleansed.
